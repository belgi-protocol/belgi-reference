## Forks and Modified Versions

If you fork either official repository and make changes, you typically **must
rename your fork** (and any distributed product, service, or package name) to
avoid user confusion about whether your version is an official BELGI™ surface.

Put plainly: you can fork the code, but you should not present a materially modified fork as “BELGI”.
